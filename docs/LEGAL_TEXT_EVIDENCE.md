# Legal Text Evidence Capability

ShanHai owns legal-text decoding, exact character spans, structural parsing,
provision-level evidence construction, legal-text capability declarations,
and citation reconstruction. It reports malformed, missing, duplicate, or
unsupported structure rather than guessing. A selector that does not identify
one provision returns an explicit unresolved or ambiguous result.

ShanHai does not establish legal authority, perform retrieval or ranking,
invoke models, ingest spreadsheets, or orchestrate cross-Skill answers. Those
concerns remain respectively unsupported, LuoHai-owned, or application-owned.
